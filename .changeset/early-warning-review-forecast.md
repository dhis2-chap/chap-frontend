Review pending alerts per organisation unit with the Modeling App's org unit menu and forecast chart, showing alert counts, alert periods and per-alert forecast ranges, thresholds and chances of exceeding them. Alert levels use the colours configured for their policy in the Modeling App. Prediction series, endemic threshold, org unit and alert level colour helpers move to the shared UI package.
