Review the signals of a prediction run on their own page: approve, decline or reopen each organisation unit's signals, one at a time or in bulk by selecting everything or one signal level. The page shows how the run's signal policy scored in the model's evaluation, and an organisation unit expands to show its forecast. The run page shows how many of its signals still await review. Export the reusable signal review table for other Chap frontends.
